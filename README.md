# 🧾 BillSplit

### Split expenses. Settle easily.

BillSplit is a user-friendly web application designed to help individuals, groups, friends, families, and organizations manage shared expenses and calculate how much each participant should contribute.

The application makes it easier to track expenses, identify who paid for what, calculate individual shares, and determine who should pay whom to settle the bill.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Shared expenses can become confusing when several people contribute different amounts toward a common bill.

Without a proper system, groups may experience:

- Confusion about who paid for an expense
- Difficulty calculating each person's share
- Disagreements about who owes money
- Difficulty keeping records of previous bills
- Lack of a simple receipt or settlement summary

BillSplit provides a simple digital solution for managing these situations.

---

## 💡 What BillSplit Does

BillSplit allows users to:

- Create a bill
- Add multiple participants
- Add shared expenses
- Record who paid each expense
- Calculate the total bill
- Calculate each participant's share
- Determine who should pay whom
- Generate a detailed receipt
- Download the receipt as an image
- Share receipt information
- Save bills for future reference
- View saved bills through Bill History
- Load previously saved bills
- Manage and delete saved bills
- View summary information through the dashboard

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 👥 Participant Management
Users can add multiple participants to a bill and remove participants when appropriate.

### 💰 Expense Management
Users can record:

- Expense description
- Expense amount
- Person who paid

Expenses can also be edited or deleted.

### 🧮 Automatic Calculation
BillSplit automatically calculates:

- Total expenses
- Number of participants
- Equal share per participant
- Individual balances
- Settlement transactions

### 🧾 Receipt Generation
After calculating a bill, users can generate a detailed BillSplit receipt containing important bill information, expenses, participants, and settlement details.

### 🖼️ JPG Receipt
Users can download the generated receipt as a JPG image.

### 📤 Receipt Sharing
Users can share receipt information through supported sharing options and social platforms.

### 📚 Bill History
Saved bills are stored in the browser so users can return to previous bills.

### 📊 Dashboard
The dashboard provides an overview of saved bills, total amounts and participants represented across saved bills.
